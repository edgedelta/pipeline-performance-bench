Pipeline Performance Benchmarks

Solid bars sustained the offered load. Dashed bars fell short; their labels show achieved requests/sec. N/A means no verified measurement. All tiers are tested independently. One 60-second trial per case; CPU includes processing through final delivery, divided by the 60-second offered-load window, including any final request and delivery drain. Memory is final RSS, not peak usage. These are fixed-load comparisons, not maximum-capacity estimates.

512 spans/request form 256 complete two-span traces. Trace IDs are unique across requests. Filter drops health traces (25%); Mask replaces the secret attribute; Sample retains approximately 50% by trace ID; Tail retains error traces (25%) after a 1-second decision window, with a 4-million-trace buffer. Collectors batch Tail output on a 500ms timer to match Rust’s decision-flush cadence; actual batch sizes remain native. Resource metadata is stable across requests. Sample checks retention and matching parent/child fingerprints; it does not independently prove the exact vendor-specific retained set or rule out small delivery loss. Other scenarios verify exact output counts. Enrich is excluded.
